### Forja | Selunite Oracle · Mines of Uroy · Fogguki's Mother | ally
Fogguki's mother. An oracle from a matriarchal line of Selunite priestesses whose blood carries weight that requires no title. She did not object when Fogguki was offered to the crown. She has not been asked why.

Fogguki's closest friend and the man who now holds the succession title he didn't finish taking. They did not part as enemies. That may be the part Fogguki cannot explain.

## Commission II — The Advance Party | Team 2 · Departed First · Status Uncertain

### Morgan | Commander · Soeros | crew
Leader of the second commission and its advance departure, from the port city of Soeros. Straightforward, competent, and thoroughly unremarkable in most respects he would name. He has read Galicus's work and likes Alexandra.

### Yor | Late Great State Magistrate · Left Hand of the King | crew
Son of a wealthy Aquenta noble, in contention for Late Great State Magistrate at twenty years old. He set out for glory and to serve Lathander, and has been appointed Left Hand of the King. He does not know that his second death has already been accounted for.

### Alexandra | Arcanist · Sarangshi · Technocrat Lineage | crew
Daughter of a wealthy technocrat family from Sarangshi. Her specialisation is interrunic microthread magic — infusing lithographically inscribed orbs through necessary mediums as part of her incantations. Her ambitions are unknown, apart from her infatuation with Martin.

### Martin Santos | Troubadour · Origins Unknown | crew
Elusive, handsome, and wealthy troubadour. As quick to enter and leave a room as he is to captivate it. Origins and destinations unknown. In song, action, and word he has made it clear he points his sails only for Alexandra.

### Dotty | Commission Member · Vessel Second | crew
Equal parts vicious, equal parts gentleman. Dotty has a crazed look that can unsettle the most steadfast with mere sustained eye contact. Fogguki knows no such reserves upon meeting his gaze.

## Persons of Interest | Notable Figures & Irregular Characters

### Henri | Cambion · Thierry Henri · Whereabouts Unknown | shadow
A cambion known to the party by first name and by the shape of the damage he leaves. His full name is Thierry Henri. The records that would formally account for his role in the trial suffer from the same selective amnesia as the records that would account for Mark. He has not been encountered since. The entity he represents has not introduced itself.

### King Charles | Sovereign · Moricci · Western Seaboard | shadow
The reigning sovereign of Moricci and the authority behind both commissions. His ambitions in the western expanse have not been disclosed to those he sent into it. His name travels ahead of him in ways he has arranged.

### Captain Tom | Drow Captain · Western Approaches · Identity Unclear | shadow
The captain who facilitated the crossing. His name is Tom in the way that all such names are Tom — a working title, issued by particular hands for a particular arrangement. He has not been seen since departure.

## Companions & Familiars | Beasts, Allies & Unlikely Friends

### Zacariah | Donkey · Property of Galicus | crew
The donkey in Galicus's keeping, acquired before the crossing and technically still alive. Zacariah cannot speak, but he can understand — a fact made known to him regularly by a ranger who exercises his gift in that direction. He has sought help from everyone he has encountered. No one has listened.

### Jocasta | Immortal Bird · Belnan's Familiar | crew
An immortal bird that arrived in Belnan's possession on the Queen's island — though possession overstates the arrangement. Where the albatross of the crossing was a thing of open sky and restraint, Jocasta is what answers when restraint is refused. She has not been tamed. She does not appear to require it.

### Kermit | Frog · Island of the Succession | crew
A frog. Kermit fought in no part for his Queen. He watched. He has since attached himself to the company on the understanding — held privately, shared with no one — that returning to that island as a bullywog is the only worthwhile ambition he has left. He does not discuss Queen Luciana.
